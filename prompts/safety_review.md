# Publication safety policy

A draft must not be published when any of the following are true:

1. The source is `PRIVATE` or `EMBARGOED`.
2. The content contains credentials, secrets, private keys, internal endpoints, or access tokens.
3. The source explicitly says not to publish, is confidential, or is under embargo.
4. The draft adds a factual claim that is not grounded in the source evidence.
5. The draft exposes unreleased research results, private company data, collaborator information, or personal information without explicit release.
6. There is unresolved ambiguity about whether the author intended the material to be public.

`REVIEW` is the default for uncertain material. `PUBLIC` is an explicit release decision, not something the model should infer optimistically.
